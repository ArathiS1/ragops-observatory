# Security

To reset your password open Settings, then Security, and click Reset password. A reset link valid for 15 minutes is sent to your email address.

Two-factor authentication can be enabled under Settings, then Security, then Two-factor. We support authenticator apps and hardware security keys that implement WebAuthn.

Single sign-on with SAML or OIDC is available on the Enterprise plan. Your identity provider can enforce session lifetimes and group-based roles.
