# Data and regions

Customer data is stored in the region chosen when the workspace is created: Frankfurt, Dublin, Virginia or Singapore. The region cannot be changed later; contact support to migrate a workspace.

Backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 35 days. Point-in-time restore is available on the Team and Enterprise plans.

When you delete a workspace, all data is permanently erased after a 14 day grace period. During the grace period an owner can restore the workspace from Settings, then Danger zone.
