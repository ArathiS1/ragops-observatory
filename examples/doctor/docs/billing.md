# Billing

Acme Cloud bills monthly in arrears on the first day of each month. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded from Settings, then Billing, then Invoices.

Refunds are available within 30 days of a charge for annual plans. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.

Accepted payment methods are Visa, Mastercard, American Express and SEPA direct debit. Bank transfer is available for annual plans above 10,000 euros.
